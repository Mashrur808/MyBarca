About Barcelona

FC Barcelona was founded in 1899 by Joan Gamper and has grown into one of football's most recognised clubs. The club is traditionally associated with the colours blaugrana and the motto "Més que un club" — "More than a club".

Barça has developed a distinctive football identity and is also known for La Masia, its youth academy. Over the decades, generations of players have shaped the club's history.

Barça History 📜

1899 — Foundation

FC Barcelona was founded in 1899, with Joan Gamper playing a major role in the club's early development.

1919–1930 — A Golden Age

During this period Barça grew significantly and began collecting major honours while establishing itself as an important football institution.

1950–1961 — The Kubala Era

The era of Ladislao Kubala brought major attention to the club. Camp Nou opened in 1957.

1988–1996 — The Dream Team

Under Johan Cruyff, Barça developed the famous Dream Team and won its first European Cup in 1992.

2008–2020 — A Historic Era

Barcelona enjoyed one of the most successful periods in its history, with a distinctive possession-based style and several major international trophies.

2021–Present — A New Horizon

A new generation of players has continued the club's story, with young talents from La Masia becoming an important part of Barça's identity.
